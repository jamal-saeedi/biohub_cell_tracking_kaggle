Created when running (paths from SETTINGS.json and the training command lines):

data/
├── train/                      competition training data
│   ├── <stem>.zarr             one 3D time-lapse movie
│   └── <stem>.geff             its annotated lineage
└── test/
    └── <stem>.zarr
models/                         MODEL_DIR, filled by tools/download_models.py
├── mres-fin-444322/1/          variant fin (the winning submission)
│   ├── isotropic_lineage/<run>/best.pt     6 networks
│   └── rescorers/MRES-FIN-444322-m<k>.npz  6 member-own re-scorers
└── mres-best-444322/1/         variant best, same layout
outputs/
├── submission.csv              SUBMISSION_DIR
├── work/                       WORK_DIR: per-movie predictions
├── training/<run>/             biohub-train: best.pt, last.pt, history.json
├── pseudo_labels/<set>/<stem>.npz          biohub-pseudo-labels
└── rescorers/<name>.npz        biohub-train-rescorer
